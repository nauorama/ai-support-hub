## Who can ask

A limit increase is possible only for Verified and Business accounts.

Starter accounts cannot ask for a limit increase. If you try, the app shows the error code ERR-5020. To get higher limits on a Starter account, move to the Verified tier.

## How to ask

1. Contact us in the in-app chat or by email.
2. Tell us which limit you want to increase.
3. Send proof of income with your request.

## How long it takes

The review takes 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## Who decides

A person in our Compliance team reviews your request and makes the decision. The AI assistant and the support team cannot approve a limit increase. We cannot promise that your request will be approved.

## Moving from Starter to Verified

The Verified tier has higher limits than Starter. For example, you can receive €30,000 per month instead of €1,000. You need:

1. proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement not older than 3 months,
2. proof of source of income.

The KYC review takes 1–2 business days.

## Until a decision is made

Your current limits stay in place. Daily limits reset at 00:00 CET and monthly limits on the 1st day of the month.
