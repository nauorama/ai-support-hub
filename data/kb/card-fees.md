## Card fees at a glance

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Virtual card | free |
| Physical card delivery, standard | €9.90 |
| Physical card delivery, express | €24.90 |
| Annual card fee | €24, charged from the second year |
| ATM withdrawal | €2.50 per withdrawal |
| Card payment in a currency you do not hold | conversion fee |

## Annual card fee

The first year is free. From the second year, the annual card fee is €24.

## ATM withdrawals

Each ATM withdrawal costs €2.50. The ATM limit is €1,000 per day (Verified) or €2,000 per day (Business).

## Paying in another currency

A card payment is made in the merchant's currency. If you hold that currency (EUR, USD or GBP), there is no conversion. If you do not hold it, the amount is converted at the mid-market rate plus 2.0%. On the Business tier the conversion fee is 1.5%.

## Fixed fees from a USD or GBP balance

The card fees above are set in EUR. When we take them from your USD or GBP balance, we convert the EUR amount at the mid-market rate, without the conversion fee.

## Replacement cards

A replacement card is a new delivery. The delivery fee applies again: €9.90 standard or €24.90 express.
