## What the card is

The Tallybridge Card is a Mastercard debit card. It spends money from your Tallybridge balance. You can have virtual cards and physical cards.

## Who can get a card

| Tier | Cards |
|---|---|
| Starter | no card |
| Verified | 1 physical card + 3 virtual cards |
| Business | 5 physical cards + 20 virtual cards |

If you have a Starter account, move to the Verified tier to get a card. You need proof of address and proof of source of income.

## Card limits

| | Verified | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Card spend per day | €5,000 | €15,000 |
| ATM withdrawal per day | €1,000 | €2,000 |

Limits are in EUR equivalent. The daily limits reset at 00:00 CET.

## Card fees

- Virtual card: free.
- Physical card delivery: €9.90 standard, €24.90 express.
- Annual card fee: €24, charged from the second year.
- ATM withdrawal: €2.50 per withdrawal.

## Paying in other currencies

A card payment is made in the merchant's currency. If you do not hold that currency, the amount is converted at the mid-market rate plus the conversion fee: 2.0%, or 1.5% on the Business tier.

## Where the card does not work

The card cannot be used for these merchant categories: gambling, crypto exchanges and adult content. A payment there shows the error code ERR-3005.

## Keep your card safe

You can freeze and unfreeze your card in the app at any time. Tallybridge staff never ask for your full card number, CVV or PIN.
