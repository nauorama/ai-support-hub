## Receiving money

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Receive EUR by SEPA | free |
| Receive GBP by Faster Payments | free |
| Receive USD by ACH | 1% of the amount |
| Payment Request paid by card | 2.9% + €0.30 |
| Payment Request paid by bank transfer | 1%, maximum €10 |

## Sending and converting money

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Withdrawal by SEPA | €1.50 |
| Withdrawal by SWIFT | €15 |
| Currency conversion | mid-market rate + 2.0% (Business: + 1.5%) |
| Mass Payouts (Business only) | free |

## Card

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Virtual card | free |
| Physical card delivery | €9.90 standard, €24.90 express |
| Annual card fee | €24, charged from the second year |
| ATM withdrawal | €2.50 per withdrawal |
| Card payment in a currency you do not hold | conversion fee as above |

## Account

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Account opening and closing | free |
| Inactivity fee | €5 per month after 12 months without transactions |

The inactivity fee never takes your balance below zero.

## How fixed fees are charged

Fixed fees are set in EUR. These are the fees for withdrawals, card delivery, the annual card fee, ATM withdrawals and inactivity. When we take a fixed fee from your USD or GBP balance, we convert the EUR amount at the mid-market rate, without the conversion fee. For example, a SWIFT withdrawal in USD costs €15 taken from your USD balance, not "15 USD".

## How percentage fees are charged

Percentage fees are charged in the currency of the payment. These are the fees for receiving USD by ACH, Payment Requests and currency conversion.

## Failed withdrawals

A failed or returned withdrawal costs nothing. The withdrawal fee is not charged.
