## Review times

| Check | Tier | Review time |
|---|---|---|
| KYC (identity) | Starter, Verified | 1–2 business days |
| KYB (business) | Business | 3–5 business days |

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## Who makes the decision

Our Compliance team reviews your documents and makes the decision. The AI assistant and the support team cannot approve or reject a verification, and cannot tell you the result in advance.

## While you wait

Until your first identity check is finished, your account cannot receive, hold or send money. You can log in and upload documents. This is required by EU anti-money-laundering rules.

## If the review takes longer

1. Check your messages in the app and your email. We may need another document.
2. Check if the app shows an error code for one of your documents, for example ERR-4001 (document unreadable).
3. If the review time has passed and you have no message from us, contact us.

## After a rejection

If a document is rejected, the app shows an error code. Upload a new document that fixes the problem. Our Compliance team then reviews the new document.
