## Only humans handle complaints

Formal complaints are handled only by humans. When triage marks a ticket as a complaint or a legal matter, the AI hands it off and does not draft a final answer.

## Deadline

A formal complaint gets a **written final answer within 15 business days**. This is an EU payment-services rule. Business days are Monday to Friday without TARGET closing days. Count from the day the complaint was received.

## How to recognise a complaint

A message is a formal complaint when the customer:

1. says it is a complaint or a formal complaint, or
2. says they are unhappy with how Tallybridge handled their case and wants it reviewed, or
3. mentions a regulator, an ombudsman or legal action.

If you are not sure, ask the customer if they want to make a formal complaint.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge the complaint and tell the customer that the written final answer comes within 15 business days.
2. Collect the facts: dates, amounts, error codes, previous tickets.
3. If another team must act (dispute, fraud, bug, limit request), open a Jira issue.
4. Write the final answer. It must be in writing and answer each point of the complaint.
5. Record the date of the final answer in the ticket.

## Special cases

- **Restricted account:** the complaint answer follows the tipping-off rule. Use only the allowed message about the review.
- **Fee refund requests:** follow the goodwill rules (€25 per case, above that a supervisor approves).
- **Fraud:** follow the fraud checklist first; the complaint runs in parallel.

## What not to promise

Do not promise a refund, a limit increase, a dispute outcome or a verification decision in a complaint answer unless that decision has already been made by the responsible team.
