## Who we are

Tallybridge is a cross-border payments platform for freelancers and small businesses. We are licensed in the EU as an electronic money institution. We have about 180,000 active customers in 60 countries.

Most customers receive money from foreign clients and marketplaces. Then they withdraw it to a local bank account or spend it with the Tallybridge Card.

## Countries where we cannot open accounts

There are some countries where Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts. If you live in one of these countries, we cannot open an account for you.

If you see the error code ERR-4020, the country on your application is one where Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts.

## Language

Our app and our support are in English.

## Currencies

You can hold and receive three currencies: EUR, USD and GBP. Each currency has its own local receiving details. See the article about the multi-currency account for details.
