## SEPA withdrawals

SEPA is for EUR withdrawals to a bank account in the EU.

A SEPA withdrawal arrives in 1 business day if you request it before 15:00 CET. CET means Central European Time, including summer time (CEST).

A SEPA withdrawal costs €1.50.

## SWIFT withdrawals

SWIFT is for all other withdrawals: USD, GBP, and EUR to a bank outside the EU.

A SWIFT withdrawal takes 2–5 business days. A SWIFT withdrawal costs €15.

## What a business day is

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar). Weekends and these closing days do not count.

## My withdrawal is late

1. Check the status of the withdrawal in the app.
2. Count the business days since you requested it. For SEPA, check if you requested it before 15:00 CET.
3. If the app shows an error code, see what it means in the list of error codes. For example, ERR-2003 means the beneficiary bank rejected the withdrawal.
4. If the normal time has passed and there is no error code, contact us. Include the amount, the currency and the date of the withdrawal.

## If a withdrawal fails

A failed or returned withdrawal goes back to your balance in 3–7 business days. You do not pay the withdrawal fee for it. See the article about failed and returned withdrawals.
