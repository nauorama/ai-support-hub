## Withdrawal limits per tier

| | Starter | Verified | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withdraw per day | €500 | €10,000 | €50,000 |
| Withdraw per month | €1,000 | €30,000 | €250,000 |

Limits are in EUR equivalent. A withdrawal in USD or GBP counts with its value in EUR.

## When the limits reset

- The daily limit resets at 00:00 CET.
- The monthly limit resets on the 1st day of the month.

## ERR-2001: daily limit reached

Your withdrawals today have reached the daily limit of your tier.

1. Wait until 00:00 CET and try again, or
2. Withdraw a smaller amount that fits in what is left of today's limit.

## ERR-2002: monthly limit reached

Your withdrawals this month have reached the monthly limit of your tier. You can withdraw again from the 1st day of the next month.

## Higher limits

- **Starter:** move to the Verified tier. You need proof of address and proof of source of income.
- **Verified and Business:** you can ask for a limit increase. Send a request with proof of income. The Compliance team reviews it in 3 business days and makes the decision.

We cannot promise that a limit increase will be approved.
