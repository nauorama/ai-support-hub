## What happens to your money

If a withdrawal fails or the receiving bank returns it, the money goes back to your Tallybridge balance. This takes 3–7 business days.

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## No fee for a failed withdrawal

A failed or returned withdrawal costs nothing. We do not charge the withdrawal fee (€1.50 for SEPA, €15 for SWIFT) for it.

## Common error codes

- **ERR-2003:** the beneficiary bank rejected the withdrawal.
- **ERR-2020:** the receiving bank returned the withdrawal.
- **ERR-2010:** the IBAN is invalid.
- **ERR-2011:** the SWIFT/BIC details are invalid.
- **ERR-2015:** the name of the beneficiary does not match the account holder. You can only withdraw to a bank account in your own name.

## What to do

1. Wait until the money is back in your balance.
2. Check the bank details with your bank: account holder name, IBAN or account number, and SWIFT/BIC.
3. Make sure the bank account is in your own name.
4. Create the withdrawal again with the correct details.

## If the money is not back

If 7 business days have passed and the money is not back in your balance, contact us. Include the amount, the currency, the date of the withdrawal and the error code.
