## When the inactivity fee applies

If your account has no transactions for 12 months, we charge an inactivity fee of €5 per month.

## Your balance never goes below zero

The inactivity fee never takes your balance below zero. If your balance is lower than the fee, we take only what is left. If your balance is zero, we take nothing.

## Fee in other currencies

The inactivity fee is set in EUR. If we take it from your USD or GBP balance, we convert €5 at the mid-market rate. We do not add the conversion fee.

## How to avoid the fee

1. Use your account at least once in 12 months, for example receive a payment or make a card payment.
2. If you no longer need the account, withdraw your balance and close the account. Closing an account is free.

Account closure takes 30 days after your request. The balance must be withdrawn first.
