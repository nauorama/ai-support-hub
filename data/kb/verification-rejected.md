## ID document problems

- **ERR-4001: document unreadable.** Upload a new, clear photo or scan. The whole document must be visible.
- **ERR-4002: document expired.** Upload a document that is still valid.
- **ERR-4003: document type not supported.** Use a passport, a national ID card or a driving licence from the EU or UK.
- **ERR-4004: driving licence not from the EU or UK.** We accept driving licences only from the EU or UK. Use a passport or national ID card instead.

## Selfie problems

**ERR-4006: selfie does not match.** The selfie does not match the photo on your ID document. Take a new selfie with your face clearly visible.

## Proof of address problems

- **ERR-4010: proof of address too old.** The document must not be older than 3 months. Upload a newer one.
- **ERR-4011: wrong type of document.** Proof of address must be a utility bill or a bank statement.

## Source of income

**ERR-4012: proof of source of income missing.** The Verified tier needs proof of your source of income. Upload a document that shows where your income comes from.

## Business documents

**ERR-4015: company documents or owner details incomplete.** Upload the missing company documents or complete the details of the owners.

## Country not supported

**ERR-4020:** Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts in this country.

## After you upload a new document

Our Compliance team reviews it. KYC takes 1–2 business days. KYB takes 3–5 business days. The Compliance team makes the decision.
