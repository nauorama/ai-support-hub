## What you can do before verification

An account without a finished identity check cannot receive, hold or send money. This is required by EU anti-money-laundering rules.

Until the check is finished, you can only:

1. log in, and
2. upload your documents.

## Error codes you may see

- **ERR-5011:** your identity check is not finished. You can only log in and upload documents.
- **ERR-1008:** your account is not verified, so an incoming payment cannot be received.

## How to finish the check

1. Open the app.
2. Upload an ID document: passport, national ID card, or driving licence (EU or UK only).
3. Take a selfie.
4. Wait for the review. It takes 1–2 business days.

Our Compliance team reviews your documents and makes the decision.

## If a document was rejected

The app shows an error code, for example ERR-4001 (document unreadable) or ERR-4002 (document expired). Upload a new document that fixes the problem.

## This is not an account review

ERR-5011 is not the same as ERR-5010. ERR-5011 means your identity check is not finished yet. ERR-5010 means your account is under review. See the article about accounts under review.
