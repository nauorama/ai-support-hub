## The rule

You can withdraw money only to a bank account in your own name. A withdrawal moves money from your Tallybridge balance to your own bank account.

You cannot use a withdrawal to pay another person or company.

## What ERR-2015 means

The app shows the error code ERR-2015 when the name of the beneficiary does not match the account holder. This means the name on the bank account is not the same as the name on your Tallybridge account.

## What to do

1. Check the name on your bank account with your bank.
2. Make sure the bank account is in your own name.
3. Enter the name exactly as your bank has it.
4. Create the withdrawal again.

## If a withdrawal was rejected

If a withdrawal failed because of the name, the money goes back to your balance in 3–7 business days. You do not pay the withdrawal fee for a failed withdrawal.
