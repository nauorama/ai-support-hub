## EUR by SEPA

- A normal SEPA transfer arrives in 1 business day.
- A SEPA Instant transfer arrives in under 1 minute.

Receiving EUR by SEPA is free.

## USD by ACH

A USD payment by ACH arrives in 1–3 business days. Receiving USD by ACH costs 1% of the amount.

## GBP by Faster Payments

A GBP payment by Faster Payments arrives on the same day. Receiving GBP by Faster Payments is free.

## Payment Requests paid by card

When your payer pays a Payment Request by card, you see the payment at once as *pending*. The money is available after 3 business days.

New accounts: for your first 3 Payment Requests paid by card, the money is available after 7 calendar days.

## What a business day is

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## When a payment takes longer

Some payments are held for review. A review takes up to 3 business days, and we may ask you for an invoice or contract. The app shows the error code ERR-1012 for a payment held for review.

If the normal time has passed and you still do not see the payment, see the article "My incoming payment has not arrived".
