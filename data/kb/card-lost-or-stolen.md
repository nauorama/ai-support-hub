## Freeze your card at once

If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it in the app right now. A frozen card cannot be used for payments.

1. Open the app.
2. Go to your card.
3. Freeze the card.

Freezing works at any time, also outside our support hours.

## Contact us

After you freeze the card, contact us in the in-app chat or by email. A person from our team handles lost and stolen cards as urgent cases. Urgent cases are also checked outside our normal support hours.

## Order a replacement

A lost or stolen card must be replaced. A replacement is a new delivery, so the delivery fee applies:

- Standard: €9.90. Delivery in 7–14 business days in the EU, up to 21 business days elsewhere.
- Express: €24.90. Delivery in 3–5 business days, EU only.

While you wait, you can use a virtual card. Virtual cards are free.

## Payments you did not make

Check your recent card payments in the app. If you see a payment you did not make, tell us. You must report unauthorised transactions within 13 months. Our team opens a fraud case.

## Never share your card details

Tallybridge staff never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code. Do not send them in chat or by email.
