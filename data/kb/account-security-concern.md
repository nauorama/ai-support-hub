## Signs to watch for

You may have a security problem if:

- you see a payment or withdrawal you did not make,
- you get a 2FA code you did not ask for,
- your login is locked (ERR-5001) and you did not try to log in,
- someone asked you for your password, PIN or 2FA code.

## What to do at once

1. **Freeze your card in the app.** You can do this at any time.
2. **Contact us** in the in-app chat or by email. Tell us what you noticed.
3. Do not share your password, PIN, CVV or 2FA code with anyone.

A person from our team handles suspicious activity. These are urgent cases and are also checked outside our normal support hours.

## Payments you did not make

You must report unauthorised transactions within 13 months. Our team freezes the card and opens a fraud case.

## Remember

Tallybridge staff never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code. Not in chat, not by email, not by phone.
