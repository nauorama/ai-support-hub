## What freezing does

When you freeze your card, nobody can pay with it. Freezing is useful when you cannot find your card or you think someone else knows your card details.

You can freeze and unfreeze your card in the app at any time.

## How to freeze your card

1. Open the app.
2. Go to your card.
3. Choose to freeze the card.

## How to unfreeze your card

1. Open the app.
2. Go to your frozen card.
3. Choose to unfreeze the card.

Only unfreeze your card if you are sure it is safe.

## Payment declined with ERR-3002

ERR-3002 means the card is frozen. A payment with a frozen card is declined. Unfreeze the card in the app and try the payment again.

## Lost or stolen card

If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it at once. Then contact us. A lost or stolen card must be replaced; the replacement is a new delivery with a new delivery fee.

## Frozen, PIN blocked or cancelled

- **Frozen (ERR-3002):** you can unfreeze it in the app.
- **ERR-3003:** after 3 wrong PIN entries, the PIN is blocked. Unblock the PIN in the app.
- **Cancelled (ERR-3004):** the card cannot be used again.
