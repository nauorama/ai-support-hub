## Which currencies you can hold

Your Tallybridge account has balances in three currencies: EUR, USD and GBP. Your account can only receive these three currencies. A payment in another currency shows the error code ERR-1001.

## Your receiving details

You get local receiving details for each currency. Give your payer the details that match the currency they pay in:

| Currency | Receiving details | Payment system |
|---|---|---|
| EUR | IBAN | SEPA |
| USD | account number and routing number | ACH |
| GBP | sort code and account number | Faster Payments |

You find your receiving details in the app.

## Before you can receive money

Your account must finish the identity check before it can receive, hold or send money. Until then you can only log in and upload documents. A payment to an account that is not verified shows the error code ERR-1008.

## Moving money out

You can move money out of your balance in two ways:

1. **Withdrawal** to your own bank account. SEPA for EUR in the EU, SWIFT for everything else.
2. **Tallybridge Card** payments, if your tier includes a card. The card spends from your balance.

Business accounts can also pay many recipients at once with Mass Payouts.

## Fees for receiving

Receiving EUR by SEPA and GBP by Faster Payments is free. Receiving USD by ACH costs 1% of the amount. See the article about receiving fees for details.
