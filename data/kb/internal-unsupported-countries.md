## The rule

Tallybridge does not open accounts in countries under EU sanctions. The customer sees ERR-4020.

## Customer wording

The customer-facing text says only: Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts in that country.

Do not:

1. mention sanctions or sanctions screening,
2. name the countries on the list or confirm whether a country is on it,
3. explain how the list is made,
4. suggest a way around the rule (another address, another person's account, another country of residence).

## If the customer insists

1. Repeat that Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts in that country.
2. Do not argue about the list.
3. If the customer says their country is wrong in the application (for example, they moved), ask them to contact support with proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement not older than 3 months. Pass the case to Compliance in Jira Service Management. Compliance decides.

## Help-centre article

The external article "Where Tallybridge is available" does not name the countries. It says there are countries where Tallybridge cannot open or serve accounts.
