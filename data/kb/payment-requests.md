## How Payment Requests work

With a Payment Request you send your client a payment link or an invoice. Your client pays by card or by bank transfer. The money goes to your Tallybridge balance.

Steps:

1. Create a Payment Request in the app.
2. Send the link or invoice to your client.
3. Your client pays by card or bank transfer.
4. You see the payment in the app.

## Fees

| How the client pays | Fee |
|---|---|
| Card | 2.9% + €0.30 |
| Bank transfer | 1%, maximum €10 |

Percentage fees are charged in the currency of the payment.

## When the money is available

- **Paid by card:** you see the payment at once as *pending*. The money is available after 3 business days. For the first 3 card payments of a new account, it is available after 7 calendar days.
- **Paid by bank transfer:** the normal time for the payment system applies. SEPA takes 1 business day, ACH (USD) takes 1–3 business days and Faster Payments (GBP) arrives on the same day.
