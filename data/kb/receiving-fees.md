## Bank transfers to your receiving details

| Currency | Payment system | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| EUR | SEPA | free |
| GBP | Faster Payments | free |
| USD | ACH | 1% of the amount |

## Payment Requests

| How your client pays | Fee |
|---|---|
| Card | 2.9% + €0.30 |
| Bank transfer | 1%, maximum €10 |

## Which currency the fee is in

Percentage fees are charged in the currency of the payment. For example, the 1% fee for a USD payment by ACH is taken in USD.

## Why I received less than my client sent

If you received less than you expected, check:

1. **The fee.** USD by ACH and Payment Requests have a fee. SEPA and Faster Payments are free.
2. **The currency.** If your client paid in USD, you received USD. Check your USD balance, not your EUR balance.

If the amount is still not clear, contact us with the date, the amount and the currency of the payment.

## Currencies you can receive

You can only receive EUR, USD and GBP. A payment in another currency shows the error code ERR-1001.
