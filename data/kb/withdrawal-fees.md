## Fees

| Withdrawal | Fee |
|---|---|
| SEPA (EUR to a bank in the EU) | €1.50 |
| SWIFT (everything else) | €15 |

## Withdrawals from a USD or GBP balance

Withdrawal fees are set in EUR. When you withdraw from your USD or GBP balance, we convert the EUR fee at the mid-market rate. We do not add the conversion fee.

Example: a SWIFT withdrawal in USD costs €15, taken from your USD balance. It does not cost "15 USD".

## The fee comes on top of the amount

Your balance must cover the withdrawal amount and the fee. If it does not, the app shows the error code ERR-2004 (balance too low for the withdrawal and its fee).

1. Lower the withdrawal amount, so that the amount and the fee fit in your balance.
2. Create the withdrawal again.

## Failed or returned withdrawals

A failed or returned withdrawal costs nothing. The withdrawal fee is not charged. The money goes back to your balance in 3–7 business days.

## Mass Payouts

Mass Payouts on the Business tier have no fee.
