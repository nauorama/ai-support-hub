## Ways to contact us

You can reach Tallybridge support in two ways:

1. **In-app chat.** Open the app and start a chat. This is the fastest way for most questions.
2. **Email.** Write to Tallybridge support by email.

English is the language of our support.

## Support hours

Our support team works at these times:

- Monday to Friday: 08:00–20:00 CET
- Saturday: 10:00–16:00 CET

CET means Central European Time, including summer time (CEST). Urgent cases, such as fraud or a lost or stolen card, are also checked outside these hours.

## The AI assistant

When you start a chat, you first talk to Tallybridge's AI assistant. It can answer common questions, for example about fees, limits and how long a payment takes. It can make mistakes. You can ask for a human at any time.

Some topics always go to a person from our team. These include an account under review, fraud, formal complaints and requests to refund a fee.

## What to include in your message

A clear message helps us answer faster:

1. Describe what happened and what you expected.
2. Add the error code if the app shows one. Error codes look like ERR-2015.
3. Add the date and the amount of the payment, if your question is about a payment.

## What we will never ask you for

Tallybridge staff never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code. Not in chat, not by email, not by phone. Do not send these in your message. If someone asks you for them, it is not Tallybridge.
