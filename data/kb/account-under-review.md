## What it means

If the app shows the error code ERR-5010, your account is under review.

During the review, our team may contact you and ask for documents. A review typically takes up to 10 business days.

## What you can do

1. Check your messages in the app and your email regularly.
2. If our team asks for documents, upload them as soon as you can.

## How long it takes

A review typically takes up to 10 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## Is this the same as an unfinished identity check?

No. If your identity check is not finished, the app shows ERR-5011, not ERR-5010. With ERR-5011 you can log in and upload documents to finish the check. See the article about accounts that are not verified yet.

## Protect your account

Tallybridge staff never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code, also not during a review. If someone asks you for them, it is not Tallybridge.
