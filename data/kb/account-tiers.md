## The three tiers

Tallybridge has three account tiers. Each tier needs different checks and has different limits.

- **Starter:** for getting started. Needs an ID document and a selfie.
- **Verified:** for higher limits and the card. Needs the Starter checks plus proof of address and proof of source of income.
- **Business:** for companies. Needs company documents and details of the owners (this check is called KYB).

## Limits per tier

| | Starter | Verified | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receive per month | €1,000 | €30,000 | €250,000 |
| Withdraw per day | €500 | €10,000 | €50,000 |
| Withdraw per month | €1,000 | €30,000 | €250,000 |
| Card spend per day | — | €5,000 | €15,000 |
| ATM withdrawal per day | — | €1,000 | €2,000 |

All limits are in EUR equivalent.

## Cards per tier

- Starter: no card.
- Verified: 1 physical card and 3 virtual cards.
- Business: 5 physical cards and 20 virtual cards.

## Mass Payouts

Only the Business tier can use Mass Payouts. With Mass Payouts you pay up to 500 recipients with one file.

## Moving to a higher tier

To move to a higher tier, you upload the documents that the higher tier needs. Our Compliance team reviews them. A review for Verified takes 1–2 business days. A review for Business takes 3–5 business days. The Compliance team makes the decision.
