## What it means

Sometimes we hold an incoming payment for review before it reaches your balance. The app then shows the error code ERR-1012.

## How long it takes

A review takes up to 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## Documents we may ask for

During the review we may ask you for an invoice or a contract for the payment. This shows what the payment is for.

Steps:

1. Check your messages in the app and your email.
2. If we ask for a document, upload the invoice or contract for this payment.
3. Make sure the document shows the payer, the amount and what the payment is for.
4. Wait for the review to finish.

## If the review takes longer

If 3 business days have passed and you have not heard from us, contact us in the in-app chat or by email. Include the amount, the currency and the date of the payment.
