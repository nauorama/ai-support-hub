## When to make a formal complaint

Most problems can be solved in a normal support chat. If you are not happy with how we handled your case, you can make a formal complaint. A formal complaint is handled by a person from our team, not by the AI assistant.

## How to make a complaint

1. Contact us in the in-app chat or by email.
2. Say clearly that you want to make a formal complaint.
3. Describe what happened, with dates, amounts and any error codes the app showed.
4. Say what you would like us to do.

Do not send your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code. We never need them.

## How long it takes

You get a written final answer within 15 business days. This is a rule for payment services in the EU.

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## What happens next

A member of our team reads your complaint and looks at your case. They may contact you if they need more information. The final answer is always written and comes from a person on our team.
