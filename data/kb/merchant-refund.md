## How long a refund takes

When a merchant refunds a card payment, the refund reaches your card in 5–10 business days.

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## What to check

1. Ask the merchant for the date they sent the refund.
2. Count 5–10 business days from that date.
3. Check your transactions in the app.

## If the refund has not arrived

If 10 business days have passed since the merchant sent the refund, contact us. Tell us:

- the merchant name,
- the amount,
- the date of the original payment,
- the date the merchant says they sent the refund.

## If the merchant does not refund you

If the merchant does not answer or refuses to refund you, you can ask us to dispute the payment. You can dispute a card payment within 120 days from the transaction date. See the article about card disputes.
