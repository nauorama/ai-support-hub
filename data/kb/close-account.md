## Before you close your account

Your balance must be zero before we can close your account. Withdraw all money first. If you try to close an account that still has money, the app shows the error code ERR-5012.

Steps:

1. Withdraw the money in each currency (EUR, USD and GBP) to your own bank account.
2. Wait until every withdrawal is completed.
3. Check that all balances are zero.

Closing an account is free. There is no fee for the closure itself, only for the withdrawals you make before it.

## How to ask for closure

1. Withdraw your balance first, as described above. Check that every withdrawal is completed.
2. Contact us in the in-app chat or by email.
3. Tell us that you want to close your account.

## How long it takes

Your account is closed 30 days after your request.

## Withdrawal fees before closing

A normal withdrawal fee applies when you withdraw your balance: €1.50 for a SEPA withdrawal and €15 for a SWIFT withdrawal.
