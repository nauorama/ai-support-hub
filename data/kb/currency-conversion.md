## The rate we use

We convert at the mid-market rate and add a conversion fee:

| Tier | Conversion fee |
|---|---|
| Starter and Verified | mid-market rate + 2.0% |
| Business | mid-market rate + 1.5% |

The conversion fee is charged in the currency of the payment.

## When we convert

1. **When you convert between your balances.** For example, from your USD balance to your EUR balance.
2. **When you pay by card in a currency you do not hold.** A card payment is made in the merchant's currency. If you do not hold that currency, we convert the amount with the conversion fee.

If you pay by card in EUR, USD or GBP and you hold that currency, there is no conversion.

## Fixed fees are different

Fixed fees are set in EUR: withdrawals, card delivery, the annual card fee, ATM withdrawals and the inactivity fee. When we take a fixed fee from your USD or GBP balance, we convert the EUR amount at the mid-market rate **without** the conversion fee.

## Currencies you can hold

You can hold EUR, USD and GBP. Your account can receive only these three currencies.

## Questions about a conversion

If a converted amount looks wrong, contact us. Tell us the date, the amount and the two currencies.
