## We never ask for these

Tallybridge staff never ask for:

- your full card number,
- your CVV,
- your PIN,
- your password,
- your 2FA code.

Not in chat, not by email, not by phone. The AI assistant does not ask for them either.

## If someone asks you for them

If someone asks for these details and says they are from Tallybridge, it is not Tallybridge.

1. Do not give them the details.
2. End the conversation.
3. Contact us through the in-app chat.

## If you already shared your details

Act at once:

1. **Card details or PIN:** freeze your card in the app.
2. **Password or 2FA code:** contact us at once.
3. Check your recent transactions in the app.
4. Tell us about any payment you did not make. You must report unauthorised transactions within 13 months.

## Login protection

2FA is mandatory for every account. You get your login code from an authenticator app or by SMS. After 5 wrong passwords, your login is locked for 30 minutes.
