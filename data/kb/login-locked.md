## ERR-5002: wrong password

The password you entered is wrong. Check that you type it correctly, for example that Caps Lock is off.

## ERR-5001: login locked

After 5 wrong passwords, your login is locked for 30 minutes. The app shows ERR-5001.

1. Wait 30 minutes.
2. Try again with the correct password.
3. Then enter your 2FA code.

## ERR-5003: login code not accepted

If your password is correct but the 2FA code is not accepted, see the article about two-factor authentication.

## ERR-5010 or ERR-5011 after login

- **ERR-5011:** your identity check is not finished. You can log in and upload documents.
- **ERR-5010:** your account is under review. See the article about accounts under review.

## If you did not try to log in

If your login is locked and you did not try to log in, someone else may have tried. Contact us at once. Freeze your card in the app if you think your account is not safe.

## Never share your password

Tallybridge staff never ask for your password or your 2FA code.
