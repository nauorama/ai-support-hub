## Pending card payments are normal

When your client pays a Payment Request by card, the payment appears in the app at once with the status *pending*. You cannot use the money yet.

## When the money becomes available

- Normally, the money is available after 3 business days.
- New accounts: for your first 3 card payments, the money is available after 7 calendar days.

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar). Calendar days include weekends.

## Fee

A Payment Request paid by card costs 2.9% + €0.30. The fee is charged in the currency of the payment.

## If it takes longer

1. Count the days again: business days for normal payments, calendar days for the first 3 card payments of a new account.
2. Check the payment in the app for an error code.
3. If the app shows ERR-1012, the payment is held for review. A review takes up to 3 business days, and we may ask you for an invoice or contract.
4. If the time has passed and there is no error code, contact us with the date and amount of the payment.
