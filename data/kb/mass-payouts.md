## What Mass Payouts are

With Mass Payouts you pay many recipients at once with one file. You can pay up to 500 recipients with one file.

## Who can use Mass Payouts

Mass Payouts are only for the Business tier. Starter and Verified accounts cannot use them. If you try, the app shows the error code ERR-2030.

To use Mass Payouts, your account must move to the Business tier. This needs company documents and details of the owners (KYB). The KYB review takes 3–5 business days. The Compliance team makes the decision.

## Fees

Mass Payouts have no fee. This is a benefit of the Business tier. You do not pay a SEPA or SWIFT withdrawal fee for the payments in a mass payout file.

## Too many recipients (ERR-2031)

One file can have up to 500 recipients. If your file has more, the app shows ERR-2031.

1. Split the recipients into more than one file.
2. Make sure each file has 500 recipients or fewer.
3. Upload each file.
