## What an unauthorised transaction is

An unauthorised transaction is a payment you did not make and did not allow. For example, someone used your card or your account without your permission.

If you made the payment yourself but there is a problem with the merchant, this is a dispute. See the article about disputing a card payment.

## What to do at once

1. Freeze your card in the app.
2. Contact us in the in-app chat or by email.
3. Tell us the date, the amount and the merchant or recipient of each payment you did not make.

Never send your full card number, CVV, PIN, password or 2FA code. We never need them.

## Time limit

You must report unauthorised transactions within 13 months.

## What happens next

1. Our team freezes your card.
2. Our team opens a fraud case.
3. A person from our team handles the case and contacts you.

Fraud cases are handled only by people, not by the AI assistant. They are urgent cases and are also checked outside our normal support hours.

## A new card

If your card was lost or stolen, it must be replaced. A replacement is a new delivery: €9.90 standard or €24.90 express.
