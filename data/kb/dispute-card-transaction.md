## When you can dispute a payment

You can ask us to dispute a card payment, for example if you paid for something you did not receive. A dispute is also called a chargeback.

If you did not make the payment at all, this is an unauthorised transaction. Freeze your card and see the article about payments you did not make.

## Time limit

You can dispute a card payment within 120 days from the transaction date.

## How to ask for a dispute

1. Contact us in the in-app chat or by email.
2. Tell us the date, the amount and the merchant name of the payment.
3. Explain what went wrong.
4. Add any proof you have, for example an order confirmation or your messages with the merchant.

A person from our team checks your request before the dispute is opened.

## How long a decision takes

A dispute decision takes up to 45 days. Complex cases take up to 90 days.

We cannot tell you the outcome in advance. We will let you know when there is a decision.

## If the merchant refunds you

If the merchant agrees to refund you, a refund to your card takes 5–10 business days.

## Never share your card details

Do not send your full card number, CVV or PIN. Tallybridge staff never ask for them.
