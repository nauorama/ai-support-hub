## Starter: ID document and selfie

For the Starter tier you need:

1. An ID document.
2. A selfie.

We accept these ID documents:

- passport,
- national ID card,
- driving licence (only from the EU or the UK).

## Verified: proof of address and source of income

For the Verified tier you need the Starter documents and:

1. **Proof of address:** a utility bill or a bank statement. It must not be older than 3 months.
2. **Proof of source of income:** a document that shows where your income comes from.

## Business: company documents and owners (KYB)

For the Business tier you need company documents and details of the owners of the company. This check is called KYB.

## Tips for a good upload

1. Make sure the whole document is visible and easy to read.
2. Use a document that has not expired.
3. For the selfie, make sure your face is clearly visible.
4. Check the date on your proof of address.

## Common problems

- ERR-4001: document unreadable.
- ERR-4002: document expired.
- ERR-4003: ID document type not supported.
- ERR-4004: driving licence not from the EU or UK.
- ERR-4006: selfie does not match.
- ERR-4010: proof of address too old.
- ERR-4011: proof of address is not a utility bill or bank statement.
- ERR-4012: proof of source of income missing.
- ERR-4015: company documents or owner details incomplete.

See the article about rejected verification for what to do.
